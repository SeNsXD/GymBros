## Features

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🏋️‍♂️ Complete Workout Logging</h3>
      <p>Log exercises, sets, reps, weights, and personal records (PRs) with zero friction. Includes dedicated trackers for cardio sessions, daily water intake, and body weight progression.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🎵 In-Workout Spotify Integration</h3>
      <p>Full control over your soundtrack without leaving the workout screen. Swipe songs to queue, switch playlists on the fly, and access music controls directly from the rest timer countdown.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🎮 Daily Quests &amp; Leveling</h3>
      <p>Turn training into an RPG. Complete daily and weekly training quests, earn XP, maintain streaks, and level up as you stay consistent.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>⏱️ Move Time Rings</h3>
      <p>Visual time targets with dual modes: <b>Reach Goal</b> or <b>Stay Under</b> (session time limits). Rings adapt in real time to your active session pace and daily activity percent.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>☁️ Encrypted Cloud Backup</h3>
      <p>Sync your routines, workout history, and personal records securely to your private Google Drive AppData folder with client-side encryption.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🎨 Modern Material 3 Design</h3>

    </td>

## Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Android 12+ (SDK 31+)
- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI Toolkit:** Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
- **Audio Integration:** Spotify App Remote SDK + Spotify Auth
- **Storage & Backup:** Google Drive REST API (`appDataFolder`) + Coroutines Play Services
- **Architecture:** MVVM with Kotlin Coroutines &amp; Flow
