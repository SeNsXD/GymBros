# 🔥 GymBros — Version Ignite

**Train. Track. Level Up. Repeat.**

GymBros is a modern Android fitness companion built to make workout tracking feel less like homework and more like part of the experience.

Track your training, chase PRs, complete Daily Quests, level up, monitor your progress, control your music, and keep your fitness data together in one place.

---

## ✨ Features

### 💪 Workout Tracking
- Track sets, reps and weight
- Strength, cardio and timed exercises
- Previous workout values
- Automatic rest timer
- Exercise reordering
- Saved workout layouts
- Workout history
- Personal Record tracking

### 🎯 Daily Quests
Complete daily fitness challenges and earn XP.

- Workout challenges
- Running & cardio goals
- Calorie challenges
- PR challenges
- Workout music challenges
- XP rewards
- Level progression
- Rest Day support

### 📊 My Stats
See your training journey in one shareable stats card.

- Workouts completed
- Total PRs
- Training time
- Calories burned
- Songs played
- Quest Milestones
- Save and share your stats card

### 💧 Hydration
- Daily water tracking
- Custom water goal
- Quick add/remove controls
- Animated hydration indicator
- Home screen water controls

### 📈 Progress Tracking
- Weight check-ins
- Weight graph
- Goal weight
- Progress photos
- Front, side and back photo tracking

### 🎵 Spotify Integration
Control your workout music without constantly leaving GymBros.

- Connect Spotify
- Select a playlist
- Play/pause during workouts
- Workout music controls
- Qualifying songs played during workouts are tracked

### 🏠 Customizable Home
Your most important fitness information in one place.

Rearrange sections including:

- Daily Quest
- Workout Streak
- Daily Activity
- Water
- Your Tools
- Spotify

### 🧰 Fitness Toolbox
Useful fitness calculators built directly into GymBros.

- Calorie Calculator
- BMI Calculator
- Protein Calculator
- Weight-Loss Pace Calculator

### 🎒 Gym Checklist
Create a reusable checklist for the things you take to the gym and set reminders for your training days.

### ☁️ Backup & Restore
Your fitness history matters.

GymBros stores workout data locally and also provides optional cloud backup and restore functionality.

Multiple accent themes are also supported, allowing you to personalize the app.

## 🧠 Philosophy

GymBros isn't supposed to be another complicated fitness dashboard.

The goal is simple:

> **Train. Track. Level Up. Repeat.**

Make tracking fast enough that it doesn't interrupt your workout, while making progress rewarding enough that you actually want to come back tomorrow.
